What to block

You can end a line in < to mean "end of the URL".

You can use * as a wildcard which will match any string of text. ** will match any text not including slashes (/).

Examples:
  • news blocks all URLs with "news" in them
  • tiktok.com blocks all pages on TikTok
  • instagram.com/explore blocks the "Explore" page on Instagram
  • youtube.com/< blocks the YouTube home page and nothing else (the slash is necessary)
  • reddit.com/*/comments blocks, for example, reddit.com/r/nfl/comments
Examples:
  • Blocking youtube.com and exempting youtube.com/watch blocks all pages on YouTube except for individual videos (which always have youtube.com/watch in their URL)
  • Blocking facebook.com and exempting facebook.com/< will allow the Facebook homepage and block all other Facebook pages

Examples:

  • 0000-0800 will be disabled from midnight to 8 A.M., blocking all times after 8 A.M.
  • 0000-0800, 1600-2400 will be disabled from midnight to 8 A.M. and 4 P.M. to midnight, blocking only between 8 A.M. and 4 P.M.
